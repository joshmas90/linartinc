# LINART Construction Inc. — app and Project Studio privacy policy

**Draft for owner review before publication.** This describes the implementation prepared in this branch. The connected service is not yet deployed. Confirm retention, contact handling, hosting/provider arrangements and the release configuration before setting an effective date or publishing a policy URL.

## Information you choose to provide

An initial project inquiry includes your name, email address, phone number, project city or ZIP, project type, and any description, contact preference or timing you choose to add. You can send an inquiry without signing in, using Project Studio, uploading images or providing a budget.

Project Studio is optional. If you use it, you may provide planning answers, photos of your home or inspiration, links, captions, documents, and optional investment or timing expectations. The app keeps edits on your device until you choose an online save or submission. Choosing a PDF for upload sends that document when selected. Online drafts are available to authorized LINART personnel, even before you mark the brief submitted.

## Why we use it

LINART uses this information to respond to inquiries, understand the work you are considering, discuss planning and estimates, and manage project conversations. The app does not include advertising, tracking or analytics SDKs. We do not request broad photo-library access, camera, contacts or device-location permission. You select the images and documents the app can access.

## Storage, access and service providers

LINART's website host processes initial inquiries, stores a private inquiry record and forwards a notification to the LINART team through its existing email system. Technical records include request IP address, timestamps and delivery state. Email handoff is not a guarantee that a message reached an inbox.

The optional online Studio uses Supabase for authentication, database records and private files. Verification codes are delivered through LINART's configured authentication email provider. Access requires a verified email and a private invitation association; later sessions use the bound user identity. Authorized LINART team members use a private administration interface. Storage objects are not publicly accessible.

Local Studio files use iOS data protection and new Studio folders are excluded from device backups. Short-lived access credentials and inquiry receipts are kept in the iOS Keychain on the device. The app re-encodes imported photos to reduce size and remove original photo metadata. File content itself, including visible addresses or people, remains part of what you choose to upload.

## Your choices

You can skip Studio, stop planning at any time, edit your draft, choose when to save online, remove uploaded attachments, and request deletion of an online project from Studio. A deletion request closes your client access while LINART reviews and fulfills it. You may also contact **services@linartinc.com** or **609-209-7810** for access, correction or deletion assistance.

Clearing local app data removes the app's saved planning data and access credentials; it does not delete previously sent inquiries, online files, emails or copies you shared with other services. A manual PDF export goes to the destination you choose. That destination's practices govern its copy. Browser links, phone calls, emails and external websites operate under their respective privacy practices.

## Retention

LINART retains information for project communications, business records and applicable obligations, and removes it when no longer needed under its approved retention schedule. Deletion may be subject to necessary recordkeeping obligations. Backups expire through the providers' backup lifecycle; exported documents and email copies require separate handling.

**Publication gate:** the owner must approve a concrete operational retention schedule and deletion-response process, including a contact responsible for requests. No automatic bulk deletion schedule has been enabled by this implementation.

## App Store declarations to review

Collected for app functionality and linked to the project/client: name, email, phone, physical/project location, project correspondence, user identifier, selected photos, uploaded documents and other user content. Optional investment information is conservatively included as other financial information. No data is used for cross-app tracking. The source privacy manifest includes these categories; review Apple's final questionnaire against the deployed configuration. This draft does not make or submit App Store Connect declarations.
